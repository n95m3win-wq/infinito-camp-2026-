⚠️ About the 5+ day rate
  • The rate applies when your child attends 5 or more days in total across the October sessions (Oct 21–22) and the winter weeks.
  • Days added later are combined if booked by December 7 (Mon).
  • Days added on or after December 8 (Tue) are charged at the single-day rate.
  • If October days were paid at the single-day rate, the difference is adjusted when you pay for the winter days.
e.g. Join Oct 21 & 22 → book 3 winter days by Dec 7
→ 5 days in total: 5+ day rate applies
e.g. Book 2 days in November → add 3 days on Dec 5
→ 5 days in total: 5+ day rate applies
e.g. Book 2 days → add 3 days after the camp starts
→ the 3 added days are charged at the single-day rate
